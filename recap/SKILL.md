# Recap

Produce a compact handoff grounded in actual work.

Include:

- Objective and current status.
- Files or surfaces changed.
- Verification commands and outcomes.
- Open risks, blockers, and follow-up decisions.

Do not fabricate test results, PR links, deployment status, or external review status.
